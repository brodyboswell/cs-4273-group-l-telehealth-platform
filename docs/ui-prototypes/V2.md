# Revised UI prototypes

Version 2 uses flat illustration, simpler cartoon face cams, restrained typography, solid colors, and reduced decoration. These are static mockups made with the built-in image generation tool.

## Exact revision prompts

### 01-practice-room

Input: 01-practice-room.png

Use case: ui-mockup. Redesign this attached prototype to look much less AI generated and much more like an actual carefully built web app with custom simple 2D vector illustration. Preserve Teleo-like spatial layout, activity purpose, bottom session toolbar and exactly TWO circular cartoon face cams stacked on left. Radical visual simplification: solid colors, clean thin outlines, crisp Inter-style typography, precise 8px-grid alignment, standard 20px outline icons, mostly square panels with 8px corner radius. No gradients, NO painterly textures, NO realistic rendering, no sunbeams, no paper grain, no inspirational slogans anywhere, no leaf logo, no decorative leaves, no excessive plant decor, no floating hearts, no faux handwriting except actual whiteboard strokes. Cream #F7F6F2, sage #88A294, muted terracotta #C97C63, charcoal #293C35. Restrained utilitarian UI, clear negative space. The two avatars are flat 2D cartoon humans with small dot eyes, simple hair silhouettes and 2px outlines, NOT Disney/3D/anime: brown-skinned adult with curly dark hair, glasses, cream shirt, labeled "You"; light-skinned teen with short dark hair and sage hoodie labeled "Alex". Small 112px diameter cams, in a 180px left rail, plain solid avatar backgrounds. All screens use identical plain cream 56px top header, "Practice Room" text at left, activity name centered, small "Simulation" text at right. All screens use identical white 80px bottom bar with thin top border, Settings, Client control on-toggle, Activities, Snapshot on left; "Building rapport · 08:42" centered; Chat and small terracotta "End session" button on right. Make a complete landscape 16:9 desktop screen, no device frame. Text and controls should feel typeset in Figma, not an illustrated poster.
Title "Welcome room". Retain immersive illustrated room as primary navigation. Redraw room as simple flat vector scene with clear uniform outlines and large undecorated surfaces, visually like an independently illustrated educational website: rectangular window left, small blank whiteboard easel, low central bookshelf with a few unlabelled books and 3 game boxes, simple feelings poster with 6 faces, muted apricot sofa right, round table with chessboard, flat oval sage rug. One small potted plant only. No words on furniture/books/walls except interactive rectangular labels "Whiteboard", "Feelings", "Chess". No large welcome card. Furniture drawn using a few geometric shapes, modest detail, no realistic lighting, texture or material rendering.
